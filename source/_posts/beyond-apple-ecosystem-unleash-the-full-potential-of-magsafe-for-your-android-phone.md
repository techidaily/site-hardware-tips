 Android phones are great, but there are some [clear advantages to using an iPhone](https://video-screen-grab.techidaily.com/new-optimal-recording-for-virtual-meetings-top-5-equipment-lists/). Like the wealth of accessories that are available for iPhones. When it comes to MagSafe accessories, you don't have to feel left out.

 "[MagSafe](https://techno-recovery.techidaily.com/mastering-regional-adjustments-how-to-update-regions-on-your-iphone/)" was introduced with the iPhone 12 series in 2020\. Not to be confused with the [old MagSafe](https://www.reviewgeek.com/21663/add-magsafe-charging-to-your-macbook-pro-with-these-cables-and-adapters/), it's essentially just a magnetic ring built-in to the back iPhone. That ring allows you to attach all sorts of things to the back of the phone with perfect alignment. Pretty neat.

 At the time of writing, there have been three series of iPhones with MagSafe. There are now thousands of MagSafe [chargers](https://desktop-recording.techidaily.com/new-2024-approved-top-10-battle-royale-games/), battery packs, wallets, and mounts on the market. Wouldn't it be cool if your Android phone could use some of them? Well, it can, and it's shockingly easy to do.
