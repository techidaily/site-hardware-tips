## Unlock the Power of Accessibility: Step-by-Step Tutorial for Enabling Live Captions on Pixel Smartphones

Live Caption automatically adds captions for any audio playing on your phone, which can be incredibly useful in many situations. We'll show you how to use it on any Google Pixel smartphone, including the Pixel 2 or newer.

 At this writing, the [Live Caption](https://www.reviewgeek.com/49525/googles-live-caption-tool-now-transcribes-video-and-voice-calls-on-pixel-phones/) feature only supports English, but it works with videos, podcasts, phone and video calls, and more (it doesn't work with music).

 The first thing to do is check and see if you have Live Caption on your phone. To do so, swipe down from the top of the screen twice and then tap the Gear icon to open the "Settings" menu.

![Tap the gear icon.](https://static1.howtogeekimages.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/screenshot_20230824-163306-1.png) 

 Next, tap "Sound & Vibration"

![Go to "Sound and Vibration."](https://static1.howtogeekimages.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/screenshot_20230824-163323.png) 

 Scroll down and tap "Live Caption." If you don't see this, you don't have the Live Caption feature on your phone.

![Select "Live Caption."](https://static1.howtogeekimages.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/screenshot_20230824-163330.png) 

 There are two ways you can use Live Caption. If you want it to display captions automatically any time audio is detected, toggle-On the "Live Caption" option.

![Toggle on "Live Caption."](https://static1.howtogeekimages.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/screenshot_20230824-163353.png) 

 If you want it to be off but easily accessible whenever audio is playing, toggle on the "Live Caption in Volume Control" option.

![Enable "Live Caption in Volume Control."](https://static1.howtogeekimages.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/screenshot_20230824-163403.png) 

 You can also use Live Caption during phone calls. To enable this, tap "Caption Calls" and select when and how you want it to work. It's not intended to be used for calls with more than one other person.

![Go to "Caption Calls."](https://static1.howtogeekimages.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/screenshot_20230824-163417.png) 

 In the image below, you see what Live Caption looks like when you watch a YouTube video. You can drag the caption box around the screen by tapping and holding it.

![Live Caption is action.](https://static1.howtogeekimages.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/screenshot_20230824-163519.png) 

 To manually turn Live Caption on or off, press one of the Volume buttons on the side of your phone to open the "Volume" controls. Tap the Live Caption icon to turn the feature on or off quickly.

![Turn on or off live caption.](https://static1.howtogeekimages.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/screenshot_20230824-163530.png) 

 If you select "Ask Every Time" for Caption Calls, you'll see the popup shown below whenever you answer a call. Select either "Caption Call" or "Don't Caption Call." You can also select the "Don't Ask Again" checkbox if you don't want to see this popup in the future.

![Select &quot;Caption Call&quot; or &quot;Don't Caption Call,&quot; and the &quot;Don't Ask Again&quot; checkbox if you don't want to see this menu in the future.](https://static1.howtogeekimages.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2020/08/10-10.png) 

 That's it! Depending on what you choose, Live Caption will either start every time you play audio on your phone, or you can toggle it on or off, as needed.
