##  What Is an "Unknown" Number?

 We're going to be showing you how to block calls from "unknown" numbers, but what does that actually mean? In short, it blocks any call from a number that does not appear on caller ID.

 This does not mean it will block calls from numbers not included in [your contacts](https://vp-tips.techidaily.com/new-simplified-guide-to-stories-mechanics/), as it [does on iPhone](https://ai-vdieo-software.techidaily.com/2024-approved-top-video-to-audio-converters-for-mobile-devices/). Unknown numbers are "unknown" because they don't display a number for caller ID. Calls from numbers you don't know---but still appear on caller ID---are not blocked.

You don't need a Google Pixel phone to use Phone by Google. Anyone can download the app from the Play Store. You'll be prompted to [set the app as your default dialer](https://screen-mirror.techidaily.com/in-2024-how-to-screen-mirroring-nokia-g42-5g-to-pc-drfone-by-drfone-android/).

##  How to Block Unknown Callers on a Samsung Phone

 If you have a Samsung Galaxy phone, you can block unknown numbers from the default phone app in the "Block Numbers" section.

 Open the "Phone" app and---from the "Keypad" tab---tap the three-dot menu icon in the top-right.

![Tap the menu icon.](https://static1.howtogeekimages.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2021/12/2021-12-17_10-33-19.png) 

 Select "Settings" from the menu.

![Select "Settings" from the menu.](https://static1.howtogeekimages.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2021/12/2021-12-17_10-34-04.png) 

 Go to "Block Numbers."

![Open "Blocked Numbers."](https://static1.howtogeekimages.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2021/12/2021-12-17_10-34-35.png) 

 Toggle the switch on for "Block Calls From Unknown Numbers."

![Toggle on "Block calls from unknown numbers."](https://static1.howtogeekimages.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2022/01/2023-03-16_15-18-17.jpg)
