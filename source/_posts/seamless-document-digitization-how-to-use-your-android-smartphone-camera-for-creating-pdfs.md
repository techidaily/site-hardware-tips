##  How to Scan Documents on Android Using Google Drive

 Open Google Drive on your Android device and tap the floating plus button, select "Scan," and take a photo of the document. Google Drive will automatically flatten the images and convert it to black and white.

 First, open [Google Drive](https://www.anrdoezrs.net/links/3607085/type/dlg/sid/UUhtgUeUpU221613/https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.google.android.apps.docs) on your Android device and make sure you're signed in with your Google account. Tap the floating "+" button in the bottom right corner.

##  How to Scan Documents on Samsung Galaxy

 You can scan documents with a [Samsung Galaxy device](https://instagram-clips.techidaily.com/new-connecting-consciously-with-content-craftsmen-of-your-realm-for-2024/) by simply pointing the camera at a document and tapping the "Scan" button when it's detected. It's one of the many [great Samsung camera features](https://win-howtos.techidaily.com/troubleshooting-tips-for-fixing-windows-11s-persistent-black-screen-fault/). The image is not saved as a PDF, however.

 Open the default camera app on a Samsung Galaxy phone and point it at a document. Make sure the entire document is in frame. When the document is detected, it will be highlighted, and a "Scan" button will appear in the bottom corner---tap it.

![Tap the "Scan" button.](https://static1.howtogeekimages.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2016/08/2023-04-07_16-29-55.png) 

 Next, you'll have the opportunity to adjust the corners of the document. Tap "Save" when you're finished.

![Adjust corners and tap "Save."](https://static1.howtogeekimages.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2016/08/2023-04-07_16-30-04.png) 

 The image will be saved to the camera roll along with your other photos. You can [convert the image to a PDF](https://extra-approaches.techidaily.com/is-auroras-hdr-enhancing-your-viewing-experience-for-2024/) if you'd like.
