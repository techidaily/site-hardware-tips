 Apple shocked many in 2016 when it released the iPhone 7 without a headphone jack. Nowadays, it's common for phones not to have a 3.5mm port. Are there any good phones left that still have a headphone jack?

 When Apple removed the headphone jack they were making a bet that the majority of people wouldn't miss it. It turns out that's pretty much true. Phones without headphone jacks are [just as popular](https://twitter-videos.techidaily.com/hot-takes-highlight-twitters-trending-topics-for-2024/), and a lot of people have transitioned to Bluetooth earbuds.

##  Do Phones Still Have Headphone Jacks?

 The short answer is "Yes," there are still phones being made with headphone jacks. However, those phones do not wear an Apple logo. Apple has stuck to its guns about removing the headphone jack and hasn't looked back.

 So, if you really want a phone with a 3.5mm port, you have to look to the Android world. On top of that, the headphone jack has become a feature you mostly find on "mid-range" or "low-end" Android phones. The big "flagship" devices have largely abandoned the port.

 For example, the [Google Pixel 5](https://www.reviewgeek.com/59751/pixel-5-review-the-nexus-returns/) was Google's "flagship" phone for 2021\. It cost $700 and did not have a headphone jack. Meanwhile, the "mid-range" [Pixel 5a](https://www.reviewgeek.com/95243/pixel-5a-review-the-best-a-series-phone-yet/) launched later for only $450 and it included a headphone jack. Apparently, if you can afford a more expensive phone, you can afford wireless earbuds, too.

### **ASUS ROG Phone 5s** 

![Asus ROG Phone 5S on blue background](https://static1.howtogeekimages.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2021/07/asus-rog-phone.jpg?trim=1,1&amp;bg-color=000&amp;pad=1,1) 

 We said high-end phones don't have headphone jacks anymore, but that's not entirely true. You can still find them if you look at gaming phones. The [ASUS ROG Phone 5s](https://shop.asus.com/us/90ai0091-m002b0-rog-phone-5s.html) not only has the port, but it has Quad DAC.

 There's a lot that goes into how a Quad DAC works, but it basically just means there's a dedicated component in the device for converting digital audio to analog for wired headphones. The result is much better sound quality than most devices.

 Additionally, the ROG Phone 5s has a massive 7.8-inch display, powerful Qualcomm Snapdragon 888 Plus processor, 12GB of RAM, and a 6,000mAh battery. It costs around $1,000.
