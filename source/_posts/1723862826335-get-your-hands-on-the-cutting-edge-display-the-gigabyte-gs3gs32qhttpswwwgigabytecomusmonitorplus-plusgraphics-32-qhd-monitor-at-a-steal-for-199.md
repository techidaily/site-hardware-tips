 This Gigabyte monitor is 32 inches wide and built around an IPS panel with a QHD resolution, which measures 2560 x 1440px. Under optimal conditions, the refresh rate can reach as high as 170 Hz, while the response time can reach as low as 1 ms.

 The Gigabyte GS32Q has great color support, covering 100% of the sRGB color gamut and reaching a brightness of up to 300 nits. As far as video input goes, there are two HDMI 2.0 ports and one DisplayPort 1.4 port. A 3.5mm jack is included for connecting external[audio](https://tools.techidaily.com) peripherals.
