 The Asus TUF gaming VG249Q1R monitor sports a 24-inch IPS panel with an FHD resolution (1920 x 1080px). The refresh rate can reach as high as 144 Hz, but it can reach 165 Hz when overclocked. It is also capable of reaching a response time of just 1 ms.

 There are several options for video input, including two HDMI 1.4 ports and one DisplayPort 1.2 input. A 3.5mm[audio](https://tools.techidaily.com) jack is also provided for connecting external audio peripherals. Newegg's 30-day return policy supports the purchase, and it includes a warranty card with more details from Asus.
